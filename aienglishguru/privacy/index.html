AI English Guru – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 5 November 2026

Who we are

AI English Guru is published by Lebaku Enterprises. You can contact us at hello@lebakuenterprises.com.

Who the app is for

The app is for adults aged 18 and over. It is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

No account

The app has no sign-up, login or user account.

Data stored on your device only

The app stores the following on your phone:

  • The lessons you mark as practised, with dates.
  • Your first name, if you enter it.
  • Your theme choice, if you set one.

This is kept in the app's local storage on your phone. We cannot see it and it is never sent to us.

Saving progress

If you choose "Save progress", the app creates a file on your device. You decide where that file goes.

Using an AI app

When you tap Start, the app copies a lesson prompt to your clipboard and opens an AI app you already have, such as Google Gemini. You paste the prompt yourself.

Your conversation happens in that app, under that company's own privacy policy and age requirements. AI English Guru never sees, records or stores your conversations.

What we do not do

  • No ads.
  • No analytics or tracking tools.
  • No selling or sharing of personal data.
  • No data sent to our servers.

Hosting

The app's files are delivered from our website. Like any website, our hosting provider may keep standard technical logs, such as IP address and request time, for security and reliability. We do not use these logs to identify you.

Permissions

The app may use:

  • The clipboard, to copy the lesson prompt.
  • Vibration, for a short confirmation when you mark a lesson done.

It does not use the microphone, camera, location or contacts.

Deleting your data

Uninstall the app, or clear its storage in your phone's Settings. This removes everything the app stored. Any progress file you saved stays wherever you put it, and you can delete it yourself.

Security

Because your data stays on your device, it is protected by your phone's own security.

Changes

If we update this policy, we will change the effective date on this page.
